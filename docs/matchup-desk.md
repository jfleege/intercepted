# Dedicated Matchups page

`#matchups` is a routed page, rather than a dashboard scroll target. The dashboard's slate and popup remain usable. Both pages share the saved-week state and search; the Matchups page selects games in its own slate and renders a report inline.

The report contains saved scoring estimates/ranges, all saved winner models, data-derived comparison statements, passing/rushing offense/defense EPA, offensive passing/rushing touchdown rates, drive outcomes, quarter/half points, EPA trends, situational rates and team production. The individual player outlook and validation report can be expanded. Current odds stay unavailable in archived weeks.

Power lists use **season offensive EPA/play descending** and **defensive EPA allowed/play ascending**, weighted by valid pass/run plays. These are efficiency rankings, not opponent-adjusted power forecasts. All teams with usable season games are eligible. Ties share competition ranks. Logos use the existing team PNGs. The green ▲ and red ▼ show prior cutoff rank minus current cutoff rank; unchanged is a neutral dash and a missing prior rank is unavailable. Weekly movement compares statistics through week W−1 with statistics through W−2, rather than inventing changes from UI refreshes.

`python -m src.ml.export_team_insights` uses the current prediction slate's season/week and completed regular-season games strictly before it. PBP final cumulative scores must reconcile with the schedule for every eligible game; an incomplete export fails rather than silently changing the league rankings. EPA denominators count nonmissing EPA plays; passing/rushing splits use play_type pass/run. Null denominators stay null.

Offensive drives group fixed_drive within game and possession team, including kneels/spikes, punts and field goals but excluding kickoffs and extra points. TD drives require an offensive rushing/passing TD; FG drives require a made field goal. Red-zone TD rate is the share of offensive drives that reach the opponent's 20 and subsequently score a rushing/passing TD. Third-down rate counts converted/(converted+failed) on the selected pass/run plays. Turnovers are plays with interception or fumble lost, counted once. Passing TDs are not counted again as receiving TDs. Defensive/special-teams return TDs are excluded from offensive TD charts.

Exports go to `frontend/public/team_insights/SEASON-week-WEEK.json`. Pages refresh runs this exporter after player/research exports, with no extra paid odds requests. The forecast week is part of the URL and response validation, so a missing historical file cannot display current rankings. Quarter/half charts reuse the existing score-reconciled research export. No winner models, player ranking weights, forecast archives or odds cadence change.
