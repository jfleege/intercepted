# Player Intelligence

The Players route uses the current saved slate, player directory export, matching research projections, watch rankings, defensive summaries, and verified research grades. It adds no sportsbook calls. Search and team/position filters narrow a compact directory. Five/ten watch cards reuse the dashboard ranking, with projection/form blending when eligible data is available. Selected profiles expose history, usage, opponent context, accuracy, and comparisons of up to three players.

`python -m src.features.player_intelligence` exports `frontend/public/player_intelligence.json` from nflverse weekly stats, final schedules, identities, and play-by-play. The morning data workflow runs it after matchup research. It reuses the current-season research PBP cache when the necessary participant fields are present; otherwise it loads current NFL PBP. No expensive training is needed for this export. Failures retain the previous file; a file from another slate is never substituted.

History includes completed regular-season appearances in the current season strictly before the forecast week. Team changes preserve original history teams, while directory identity uses the latest observed team and tracked matchup overrides. This is a research directory, not a confirmed active roster or injury feed. Projection-only players can appear with no history. Missing stats stay null. Last 5/10/season averages use available appearances, not invented zero games or the watch card's three-game average.

Usage is actual attempts/carries/targets and receptions from weekly data. Period usage uses explicit passer/rusher/receiver IDs and attempt/completion flags, excluding no-play, kneel, and spike rows. A game must have all four regulation quarters in its PBP feed. Quarter/half totals are summed over the selected available appearances; OT is separate. Touches are carries plus receptions, not targets. Snap share is unavailable and labeled accordingly.

Historical projected-vs-actual markers use only the original verified saved yardage forecast for the matching player, target, and game. No current forecast is drawn backward onto old results. Reliability and the accuracy table use published verified graded rows of the same player, target, season and optional weight version. Last 5/10/season controls also limit graded accuracy samples after the version selection. MAE excludes ungraded/missing outcomes; range coverage uses only rows with range evidence. All-version summaries are descriptive, with a mixed-version note. Small samples are labeled and are not a recalibration verdict. Latest graded outcomes can include the displayed forecast week, while pregame usage/form history remains through the prior week.

QB scoring-TD and passing-TD probabilities are separate events. TD estimates remain conditional on appearing. Opponent ranks are team-level yards allowed, not receiving-position matchup scores; rank 1 allows the fewest. Player red-zone matchup rates and snap share remain explicit missing-data states. Comparisons preserve differing yardage types and never treat passing, rushing and receiving scales as interchangeable.

Validation includes cutoff/period attribution tests, directory/source/version/reliability tests, and browser checks at 375/650/900/1440px for search, filters, history windows, period views, QB events, comparison, missing feeds, and navigation.
