# Likelihood-based Markets

Markets loads predictions and same-slate matchup research. It does not request odds.json, markets.json, player_props.json, or odds_usage.json. Matchup/dashboard sportsbook displays and the existing daily game-odds refresh remain unchanged. Automatic player-prop requests are disabled. The old explicit manual workflow input and capped exporter are retained for recovery/research, but their cache is unused by Markets. Old tracked caches and quota reservations are retained; no new provider calls are made by this change.

Winner estimates use the selected saved winner model and the predictions export timestamp; touchdown estimates use the saved conditional classifier. Player identity and same-slate checks remain in place. Unknown availability stays unknown; fresh sourced Out players are excluded from generated ideas and cannot be added or saved into a new plan. No model is retrained or regraded.

Yardage, totals, and spreads remain editable/derived mean-and-range guides. No distribution is inferred from a mean or an 80% range, and no hit probability is invented. They cannot enter automatic cross-game ideas. No price, payout, EV, bankroll, or Kelly recommendation appears in Markets. Those require prices as well as defensible probability estimates.

Decision Lab applies a user-selected percentage-point reduction to each known probability, clamped at zero. The threshold classification compares the original and reduced estimates against the user's chosen individual-likelihood floor. This is a descriptive sensitivity screen, not a confidence interval, calibration adjustment, profitability claim, or recommendation to bet.

Cross-game controls select probability profile, 2–4 legs, 3–6 alternatives, winners/TD/both, and included games. Global search also applies. Higher means >=65%, near-even 45–55%, longer <45%; agreement uses the fraction of winner models favoring the actual selected side (not the home team). Unknown, future-dated, post-kickoff, and missing-time forecasts cannot generate ideas. A bounded deterministic beam search keeps up to six candidates per game and 240 partial combinations at each depth. Results are heuristic research alternatives, not exhaustive globally optimal portfolios. An overlap penalty encourages variation; alternatives may share selections. Each has exactly one leg per game, unique canonical identities, and the requested count; insufficient pools return fewer options or an empty state, never repeated/fabricated legs.

For winner-only plans, Frechet bounds are max(0, sum(p)-(n-1)) to min(p). These are mathematical bounds given the supplied marginals, not confidence intervals or a fitted joint model. An off-by-default independence scenario displays product(p) only when explicitly enabled; different games do not prove independence. Any selection without a known probability makes a joint summary unavailable. TD estimates are conditional on individual appearances, which differ across players; mixing those conditionals or with unconditional winner estimates does not establish common marginals. Any TD-containing combination therefore withholds joint bounds and independence products.

New plans use `intercepted.likelihood-plans.v1`. The original `intercepted.projection-slips.v1` is only read, never rewritten. Earlier drafts can be exported losslessly or loaded as historical selections with quote fields stripped. New plans contain no quotes, prices, stakes, or asserted joint probability. Loading flags historical estimates rather than silently revaluing them. Corrupt storage is preserved and new writes to that store are blocked.
